# κ(T) for both materials, 30–700 K

Each figure is generated by `plot_kappa_vs_T.py` from `data_mnin2se4.json` /
`data_mnin2se5.json` (both carry their own provenance). kappa_zz is plotted as
the **rest of the zone** at every temperature.

| file | contents |
|---|---|
| `MnIn2Se4_kappa_vs_T.png/.pdf` | in-plane and κ_zz(rest) vs T, 300 K marked |
| `Mn2In2Se5_kappa_vs_T.png/.pdf` | same |
| `both_kappa_vs_T.png/.pdf` | both materials on the same axes |

## How the data were produced

Two RTA runs per material, `write_kappa=True`:

| material | mesh / FC3 | 100–700 K | 30–150 K |
|---|---|---|---|
| MnIn₂Se₄ | 14³ / 6.45 Å / isotopes on | job 416235 | job 416310 |
| Mn₂In₂Se₅ | 16³ / 6.51 Å / isotopes off | job 416236 | job 416311 |

Then each temperature was decomposed with
`kappa_zz_decompose.py --temp-index i` so that κ_zz is the rest-of-the-zone term
rather than the contaminated total.

**1 K was computed but is excluded**: the Bose occupation diverges, κ_zz comes out
at 19109 (MnIn₂Se₄) / 7823 (Mn₂In₂Se₅) W m⁻¹ K⁻¹, i.e. pure numerical
divergence, not physics. 30 K is the lowest usable point.

## Read this before quoting anything below 150 K

The sub-150 K region is **shaded** in the figures because those points are
mesh-limited: the acoustic mean free paths there exceed what a 14³/16³ mesh can
sample, so κ is a **lower bound**. A converged low-T curve needs either much
denser meshes or an explicit boundary/grain-size scattering term
(`boundary_mfp`), neither of which is in these runs.

300 K (green circle) is the value quoted in the reports and is mesh-converged;
its κ_zz is additionally limited by the FC3 cutoff (see
`../cutoff/README.md`).
