Dynamic stability check (all phonon frequencies on the 11^3 q-mesh, taken from the
frequency dataset of kappa-m111111.hdf5)
- all 39 fits (36 pheasy + 3 symfc) have 0 imaginary frequencies;
- most negative frequency = -3.83e-05 THz (the numerical zero of the acoustic modes at Gamma);
- highest frequency ~ 7.73 THz (practically identical across cutoffs/methods, showing that FC2
  is dense and already converged).
Conclusion: the second-order force constants of every cutoff/method are dynamically stable.
