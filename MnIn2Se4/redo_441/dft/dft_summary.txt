### 2.2 ⚠️ Band gap: **the previously reported 0.4055 eV does not hold; this is a semimetal**

The 0.4055 eV obtained from the "occupied/unoccupied" classification is an artefact that hides the
band overlap. **Verified k point by k point against EIGENVAL** (`s2_static/EIGENVAL`):

| Spin | Occupied bands per k point | Anomalous k point |
|---|---|---|
| up | 34 (19 k points) / **35 (1 k point)** | **Γ (0,0,0), w = 1/128 = 0.007812** |
| down | 29 (19 k points) / **30 (1 k point)** | the same Γ point |

At the Γ point the spin-up **two bands are degenerate**: band 34 = band 35 = **+3.9371 eV**
(spin down: band 29 = +3.3889, band 30 = +3.8039).
The occupation is redistributed at the degeneracy → one electron moves from spin up to spin down →
the magnetisation drops by 2/128 = 1/64, exactly 5 − 1/64 = 4.9844.

**Electronic-structure conclusion (from the weighted occupations, independently checked against
EIGENVAL)**:

| Item | MnIn₂Se₄ |
|---|---|
| weighted electron count up / down | **33.9922 / 29.0078** (63 in total) |
| total magnetisation | 4.98438 = **5 − 1/64** |
| up hole pocket | valence-band top band 34, E = **3.9945** (8 k points with occupation 0.9088-0.9880, 1/128 short in total) |
| up Γ-point electron pocket | band 34 = band 35 = **3.9371** (degenerate pair, occ 0.9985) |
| down Γ-point electron pocket | band 30 = **3.8039** (occupied at Γ, empty at the other k points) |
| **cross-spin overlap** | 3.9945 − 3.8039 ≈ **0.19 eV** |

**Conclusion: a compensated semimetal**. The spin-up hole pocket is compensated by 1/128 each from
the spin-up and spin-down electron pockets at Γ; the net magnetisation = 5 − 2/128 = 5 − 1/64 =
4.9844 ✓. **Neither spin channel is gapped** -- the earlier statement "spin down is gapped by
0.415 eV", made by fixing the band index, is wrong (that 3.8039 band is actually occupied at Γ: an
electron pocket, not a conduction band).

**Cross-check (Mn₂In₂Se₅ is fully isostructural)**: up/down = 45.9922 / 36.0078, magnetisation
9.98438 = 10 − 1/64; up valence-band top 4.4118 (hole pocket, occupation 0.90-0.96), Γ-point up
band 46 = band 47 = 4.3420, down band 37 = 4.2835 (electron pocket), cross-spin overlap
4.4118 − 4.2835 ≈ **0.13 eV**.

**Impact and disposition**:
- **Little impact on κ**: the forces are self-consistent; the fit error for Mn₂In₂Se₅ is only 0.4%,
  and the recomputed κ agrees with phono3py.
- **The real safeguard is the per-frame magnetisation of S4**: all frames should be ≈
  **16 × 4.9844 = 79.75 μB**; any frame that deviates had an occupation jump triggered by the
  displacement, and its forces cannot be trusted.
- **Paper**: until EIGENVAL has been verified, **do not write "an insulator with a 0.41 / 0.25 eV
  band gap"**. If the semimetal description is adopted, then **NAC=off has the better physical
  basis** (free carriers screen the LO-TO splitting).

| Born effective charges | **Mn −0.94311, In −0.62184 (negative)** | ❌ wrong sign |
| SCF | converged in 7 steps, no ERROR/WARNING, CPU 91.3 s | (the SCF itself is fine) |

**Born-charge criterion**: Mn²⁺ and In³⁺ are cations, so the correct values should be **+2 to +3**;
here they are all negative, which shows the linear response was likewise not computed.
(Mn₂In₂Se₅ behaved exactly the same way back then: ε = 1, Mn −0.90.)

**Root cause**: VASP's DFPT/LEPSILON requires **the same number of occupied bands at every k
point**; by section 2 the occupation is rearranged at Γ (one band more for up, one less for down),
so the condition is not met → the whole response fails. This is the same fact as "S2 is a
semimetal", seen from another side.

**Impact**: **the main line is not blocked** -- S6_kappa has already fixed `NAC=off` (consistent
with Mn₂In₂Se₅), and the S5_fc force-constant fit does not need the dielectric tensor either.
Should NAC be enabled in the future, this step must be solved first (e.g. drop LDAU in S3, or
switch to the LCALCEPS finite-difference route).
